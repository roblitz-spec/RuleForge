## 1. Purpose

Provide a controlled matrix that maps validated operational evidence to approved governance artifacts and readiness assessment topics within the Pre-PAC-3 Readiness framework. The matrix supports audit navigation, evidence traceability, and assessment preparation. It does not introduce new governance requirements, modify the PAC-2 Governance Baseline, alter PAC-3 entry criteria, authorize PAC-3 activities, or expand governance scope.

---

## 2. Scope

This matrix applies to validated operational evidence managed through the approved evidence lifecycle. It records traceability relationships only and complements existing evidence management records.
