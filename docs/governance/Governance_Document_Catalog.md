## 1. Purpose

Provide a centralized catalog of approved governance documents supporting the Pre-PAC-3 Readiness framework. The catalog improves document administration, configuration management, and audit accessibility while preserving the approved governance baseline. It does not introduce new governance requirements, modify the PAC-2 Governance Baseline, alter PAC-3 entry criteria, authorize PAC-3 activities, or expand governance scope.

---

## 2. Scope

This catalog applies to all approved governance documents maintained within the governance framework. It records administrative information only and does not replace the authoritative documents.
