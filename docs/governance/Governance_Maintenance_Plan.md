## 1. Purpose

Define the recurring operational activities required to maintain the approved Pre-PAC-3 governance framework following completion of governance construction. This plan establishes the routine maintenance cycle for existing governance artifacts. It does not introduce new governance requirements, modify the PAC-2 Governance Baseline, alter PAC-3 entry criteria, authorize PAC-3 activities, or expand governance scope.

---

## 2. Maintenance Objective

Maintain governance effectiveness through disciplined operational execution while preserving the approved governance baseline. Governance maintenance focuses on operational continuity rather than governance expansion.
