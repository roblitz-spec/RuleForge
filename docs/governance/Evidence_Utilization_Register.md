## 1. Purpose

Provide a controlled register for tracking the utilization of validated operational evidence within the Pre-PAC-3 Readiness framework. The register records how approved evidence is referenced during readiness assessments and governance reviews, supporting transparency, traceability, and evidence lifecycle completeness. It does not introduce new governance requirements, modify the PAC-2 Governance Baseline, alter PAC-3 entry criteria, authorize PAC-3 activities, or expand governance scope.

---

## 2. Scope

This register applies to operational evidence that has completed the approved evidence management process. It records evidence utilization only and complements existing evidence intake, verification, maturity, and status records.
