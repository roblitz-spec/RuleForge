## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the operating principles for maintaining the governance system after the governance baseline has been established. Its objective is to ensure governance remains stable, evidence-based, auditable, and continuously effective throughout normal operations.

---

## 2. Policy Statement

- The governance baseline shall be maintained as the authoritative source of governance requirements.
- Operational activities shall focus on maintaining governance effectiveness rather than expanding governance documentation.
- All governance activities shall be supported by objective, verifiable evidence and conducted through approved processes.

---

## 3. Operating Objectives

| # | Objective |
|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain governance baseline integrity |
| 2 | Ensure operational compliance with approved governance requirements |
| 3 | Preserve evidence completeness and traceability |
| 4 | Support periodic reviews, audits, and readiness assessments |
| 5 | Implement governance improvements only through formal change control |

---

## 4. Operating Principles

### 4.1 Baseline Stability

Approved governance artifacts shall remain unchanged unless modified through an authorized change process.

### 4.2 Evidence-Based Governance

Governance decisions shall be supported by objective operational evidence rather than assumptions.

### 4.3 Traceability

Governance requirements, procedures, operational records, reviews, findings, and corrective actions shall remain traceable throughout their lifecycle.

### 4.4 Controlled Change

Governance changes shall only occur following:

| Step | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1 | Change Request |
| 2 | Impact Assessment |
| 3 | Approval |
| 4 | Controlled Implementation |
| 5 | Verification |
| 6 | Baseline Update |

### 4.5 Continuous Assurance

Governance effectiveness shall be demonstrated through routine operation, scheduled reviews, and objective evidence.

## 6. Event Triggers

The governance operations agent shall respond only to predefined operational events:

| # | Trigger | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generation of new operational evidence | `GOV-REF-028` |
| 2 | Scheduled governance reviews | `GOV-REF-022` |
| 3 | Approved governance changes | This policy §4.4 |
| 4 | Audit activities | `GOV-REF-017` |
| 5 | Readiness assessments | `GOV-REF-011` |
| 6 | Operational findings requiring corrective action | `GOV-REF-021` |

**No governance activity shall be initiated without an approved trigger.**

---

## 7. Standing Restrictions

Unless explicitly authorized, the governance operations agent shall not:

| # | Restriction |
|---|---|
| 1 | Create new governance artifacts |
| 2 | Modify approved governance documents |
| 3 | Expand governance scope |
| 4 | Restructure repositories |
| 5 | Generate documentation without operational justification |

---

## 8. Governance Performance Indicators

Governance effectiveness shall be evaluated using operational indicators:

| # | Indicator | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Governance Baseline Stability | No unauthorized changes |
| 2 | Evidence Completeness | All required evidence present |
| 3 | Evidence Quality | Verified, traceable, consistent |
| 4 | Traceability Integrity | End-to-end linkage maintained |
| 5 | Scheduled Review Completion | Reviews performed per calendar |
| 6 | Corrective Action Closure | Findings resolved |
| 7 | Audit Readiness | Demonstrable at any time |

**Document quantity shall not be considered a governance performance indicator.**

## 11. Governance Success Definition

The governance system is considered successful when:

| # | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 1 | Governance remains stable |
| 2 | Operational evidence is complete and reliable |
| 3 | Traceability is maintained |
| 4 | Reviews are performed as scheduled |
| 5 | Approved changes are controlled |
| 6 | Audit readiness can be demonstrated at any time |
| 7 | Continuous improvement is evidence-driven |
