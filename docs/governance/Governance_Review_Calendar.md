## 1. Purpose

Define the recurring review schedule supporting the sustained operation of the approved Pre-PAC-3 governance framework. This calendar coordinates existing governance maintenance activities without introducing new governance requirements, modifying the PAC-2 Governance Baseline, altering PAC-3 entry criteria, authorizing PAC-3 activities, or expanding governance scope.

---

## 2. Review Principles

Governance reviews shall:

| # | Principle |
|---|---|
| 1 | Follow an established operational cadence |
| 2 | Confirm continued alignment with the approved governance baseline |
| 3 | Preserve configuration integrity |
| 4 | Validate evidence maturity |
| 5 | Support objective readiness assessments |

---

## 3. Review Schedule

| # | Activity | Frequency | Primary Output | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evidence Log Review | Monthly | Updated Evidence Log | `GOV-REF-012` |

**9 reviews. 3 cadences: Monthly (3), Scheduled Cycle (4), Event Driven (2).**

---

## 4. Review Responsibilities

Each review shall:

| # | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| 1 | Verify completion of the scheduled activity |
| 2 | Confirm supporting records remain current |
| 3 | Identify exceptions requiring action |
| 4 | Preserve cross-record consistency |
| 5 | Maintain evidence traceability |

---

## 5. Review Outcomes

A completed review may result in one or more of:

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| No Action Required | Continue maintenance |
| Record Update | Update affected operational records |
| Corrective Action | Activate exception protocol (`GOV-REF-021`) |
| Maintenance Follow-up | Schedule additional verification |
| Readiness Gate Review | Only when supported by validated evidence |

**Review completion alone shall not authorize PAC-3 progression.**

## 7. Standing Position

The governance framework shall remain under routine operational review. Existing governance artifacts provide sufficient control for the current operational scope. Expansion of governance documentation is not the default operating model.

```
Standing Governance Decision: MAINTAIN BASELINE
```

---

## 8. Conclusion

The Governance Review Calendar establishes a predictable review cadence supporting the continued effectiveness of the approved governance framework. Future readiness progression shall continue to rely on validated operational evidence and approved readiness assessments.
