## 1. Purpose

Provide a controlled operational register for recording validated evidence generated during steady-state execution of the Pre-PAC-3 governance framework. This register marks the transition from governance and administrative construction toward evidence maturation. It does not introduce new governance requirements, modify the PAC-2 Governance Baseline, alter PAC-3 entry criteria, authorize PAC-3 activities, or expand governance scope.

---

## 6. Evidence Handling

New evidence shall be:

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Recorded against its source |
| 2 | Checked for completeness and consistency |
| 3 | Verified for traceability |
| 4 | Associated with the relevant operational or assessment record |
| 5 | Retained according to the existing records-management framework |

**No new governance artifact is required merely because operational evidence is generated.**

---

## 7. Readiness Relationship

Validated evidence may support future readiness assessments when it demonstrates actual operational performance against existing approved criteria.

Evidence maturity shall be determined through existing assessment mechanisms (`GOV-REF-011`, `GOV-REF-013`).

**The register does not independently determine PAC-3 readiness.**
