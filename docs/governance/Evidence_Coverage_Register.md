## 1. Purpose

Provide a controlled register for monitoring the coverage of validated operational evidence across approved readiness assessment topics within the Pre-PAC-3 Readiness framework. The register supports visibility into evidence coverage, identifies areas requiring additional operational evidence, and assists readiness assessment planning. It does not introduce new governance requirements, modify the PAC-2 Governance Baseline, alter PAC-3 entry criteria, authorize PAC-3 activities, or expand governance scope.

---

## 2. Scope

This register applies to validated operational evidence that has entered the approved evidence management lifecycle. It records evidence coverage relationships only and complements the existing evidence intake, verification, maturity, status, and utilization records.

---

## 3. Register Structure

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Coverage Reference | Unique coverage identifier (`COV-XXX`) |
| Assessment Topic | Approved assessment topic or objective |

| Coverage Status | Not Started / Partial / Complete |
| Last Review Date | Most recent coverage review |
