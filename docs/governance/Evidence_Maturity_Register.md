## 1. Purpose

Provide a controlled register for monitoring the maturity of validated operational evidence generated during Phase 3 (Evidence Maturation). The register supports consistent evidence evaluation and readiness assessment preparation. It does not introduce new governance requirements, modify the PAC-2 Governance Baseline, alter PAC-3 entry criteria, authorize PAC-3 activities, or expand governance scope.

---

## 4. Maturity Stages

| Stage | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Initial** | Evidence accepted and verified; traceability established; first observation recorded |
| **Developing** | Repeated observation; operational consistency emerging; supporting records aligned |
| **Mature** | Sustained consistency over multiple review cycles; full traceability; independently verifiable |

---

## 5. Evidence Maturity Principles

Evidence maturity shall be evaluated based on:

| # | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 1 | Verified origin |
| 2 | Complete traceability |
| 3 | Demonstrated operational consistency |
| 4 | Repeatable observation through approved activities |
| 5 | Alignment with existing governance records |

**Evidence maturity reflects operational confidence only and does not independently determine readiness.**
